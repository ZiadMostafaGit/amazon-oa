Recommendation Algorithm
The scoring algorithm generates personalized movie recommendations based on user preferences:
User Action Recommendation Strategy Score Multiplier
Rated high (above 5) Recommend movies with simllar genres 1.2x boost
Rated low (5 or below) Recommend movies with different genres No boost
Watched only (no rating) Recommend movies with simllar genres No boost
Watched + rated high Recommend movies with similar genres 1.2x boost
Watched + rated low Recommend movies with different genres No boost
Constraints:
* Only recommend movies with a rating of 7.0 or above
« Remove duplicates, sort by score descending, return top 10
« When same movie appears from multiple sources, keep the one with the higher score
« When a movie is both rated and watched, the rated signal takes precedence
*« The userRating field is only present in recommendations where the source is "rated" , not for watched-only recommendations

totalScore = (genreScore « 9.7 + ratingScore ~ 9.3) =x multiplier
