NODE knapsack: Knapsack
TECHNIQUES:
  - 0/1 knapsack: take or skip under a capacity
  - subset sum: can I make exactly T
  - exactly W versus at most W (the initial values)
  - unbounded: each item any number of times (fewest coins)
  - count combinations versus orderings (loop order)
  - a disguise: +/- signs to reach T becomes subset sum
  - partition into two sets with the smallest difference
  - two resources at once (ones and zeroes)
  - bounded copies, or swap the DP dimension when the capacity is huge
